Why these two screens

Second screen

Your iPad is the register. During a rush it's busy taking payment, not watching for online orders. The order board is a second screen, not a Square upgrade.

Your whole recipe book

Every drink you've ever named — built in once, not re-typed each season. Halloween, Star Wars Day, Christmas, all of it lives in the system. But a customer only ever sees what's actually in season — the rest stays off the menu until its dates come back around, automatically.

The last truffle

One truffle in the fridge doesn't get sold twice. Same idea as your "ONE left!" posts — the site knows what's actually still there.
